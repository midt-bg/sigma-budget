Известни ограничения (23 набора, бележките на манифеста на английски)
  • outlook (КФП and сектор „Държавно управление“ (S.13): macro framework and fiscal targets): reads pdf_pages_clean.json, which asbp_pages.py makes from the asbp PDF (pinned in data/sources.yaml)
  • gva-sectors (national economy (НСИ via Eurostat)): the answers of 05.11.2026 give GDP 105 279,0 (2024) and 116 548,5 (2025) млн. €, outlook.json 104 767,2 and 116 018,3: rebuilding it means a new release with the revised national accounts, not a reproduction
  • expenditure (КФП by function (COFOG), Приложение 3): data/inputs/cofog_bg.json and kfp_functions_2026.json have no producer (spec, section 4)
  • components (the constituent budgets of the КФП): data/inputs/kfp_components_2026.json comes from build_src.py, which no longer runs
  • measures (КФП, discretionary measures of the 2026 budget): data/inputs/measures_2026.json has no producer (spec, section 4)
  • law (the three 2026 budget laws: bill against adopted law): the parliament API is live: a change in its counts stops the build until reviewed (law.py)
  • execution (КФП, national budget and EU funds, cash basis): data/inputs/kfp_components_2026.json comes from build_src.py, which no longer runs
  • execution-provenance (every number of execution.json, and the plan for 2026): the national and EU plan (plan2026.nat, plan2026.eu) points into data/inputs/kfp_components_2026.json, whose producer no longer runs
  • debt (government debt (ЗДД) and Maastricht debt (S.13)): data/inputs/debt_gdp.json has no producer; debt_stock.json is a snapshot of an earlier Eurostat answer
  • rates (taxes and social security contributions in force in 2026): the NAP table (data/originals/nap/) is downloaded only from Bulgaria; from elsewhere nra.bg may serve a CAPTCHA page
  • municipalities (transfers from the state budget to the 265 municipalities): municipal_transfers_2026.json and population.json come from build_src.py, which no longer reproduces them
  • geo-obshtini (municipal borders and population (LAU)): the borders are simplified from the GISCO GeoJSON, which is streamed and not kept
  • glossary (editorial): Published editorial snapshot; original supplied raw text unavailable.
  • guide (editorial): Published editorial snapshot; original supplied raw text unavailable.
  • core (the shell of the site: figures copied from the datasets): after build_core: the v5 wording, the v5 removal of СЕБРА and the generated sets, and the four S34 sources of v6 (layers)
  • search-index (the site): after search: the v5 wording and removals (layers)
  • catalog (the site): after catalog: the v5 wording and removals and catalog_refresh.py (layers)
  • sebra (МИДТ only, 27–31.08.2026): built, but neither listed in catalog.json nor loaded by a screen: three days of one ministry, kept for phase 2
  • dbp2027 (сектор „Държавно управление“ (S.13), ESA 2010): not listed in catalog.json (the catalog screen would change)
  • releases (the release of data/release: manifest.json and validation.json of every release, current.json points to the current one): the manifest is not in catalog.json: it lists the sha256 of the catalog, and the catalog would have to list the manifest; no environment has the registry yet (it needs the Cloudflare account): the originals are public only from a registry
  • budget2027 (MF publication 2026-11-06; bill and forecast are separate): Additive offline contract; no UI reader until PR 2.
  • budget2027-provenance (MF publication 2026-11-06; bill and forecast are separate): Additive offline contract; no UI reader until PR 2.
  • indirect-tax-parameters (България; ставки и датирани наблюдения, без потребителски тегла): Цените са исторически справки; изчисленията са сценарий. Без LPG и периоди преди 01.08.2026.

Всеки може да сглоби изданието отново от оригиналите и да сравни всеки файл байт по байт. Нужни са само кодът и Docker:

git clone https://github.com/midt-bg/sigma-budget.git && cd sigma-budget
./budget reproduce --current https://sigma-budget.my-data.bg

Командата чете манифеста на текущото издание, сваля оригиналите му оттук и сглобява commit-а му в закования образ. Изходът е reproduced byte for byte или списък с разликите. Подробности: docs/release.md (в нов раздел).

Произход и методика

  • Всяко число от месечните отчети води до листа и клетката на оригинала: произходът на всяко число (JSON) и „Откъде са числата“ в месечните отчети.
  • Наборите, източниците им и методиката: „Откъде са данните?“.
  • Файловете и как се четат с код: „Отворени данни и API“.
  • Публичният API на изданията: текущото издание и журналът (JSON).
